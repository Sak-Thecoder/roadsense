# Rights and provenance

## StreetSurfaceVis

The authors' [Zenodo record](https://zenodo.org/records/11449977) identifies the dataset as CC BY-SA and says the `train` flag identifies five geographically distinct test cities. Its [dataset description](https://zenodo.org/records/11449977/files/dataset_description.pdf) links the license to [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The supplied CSV MD5 is `a6ac6ab1edf7d813ebe7ffb22b922423` and guide MD5 is `598da7ba7e93fd995ff30a3de2ac03b0`; both match the Zenodo listing. This corroborates the supplied metadata and guide; individual downloaded images were not compared against official image archive checksums. The source authors say to follow Mapillary's terms. [Mapillary's license guidance](https://help.mapillary.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001770409-CC-BY-SA-license-for-open-data) requests attribution including image and contributor links. Preserve usernames, image IDs, license link, source and changes. CC BY-SA includes share-alike conditions for adaptations and may not cover privacy or other rights; assess intended dataset/model use and distribution before treating training permissions as cleared. Applicability of share-alike to a model requires a separate assessment; it is not resolved here. The output thumbnails are local review derivatives and should not be redistributed without following applicable attribution and license terms.

The provisional StreetSurfaceVis manifest records dataset-level license evidence; it does not mark intended use as fully cleared. Its mapping is not approved.
